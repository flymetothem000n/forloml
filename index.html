For Forgetting & For My Words

— a letter I owe you —

My dearest love,

I've been wanting to say this properly for a while, and I hope you'll let me. I'm truly sorry — for the times I forgot things that mattered to you, for the little promises I let slip, and for every moment my forgetfulness made you feel unimportant. You deserve to be remembered, always. You are never a small thing to me, and I'm sorry I ever made you feel that way.

I'm also sorry for my words. For the times I spoke carelessly, too quickly, or in a way that hurt you — even when I didn't mean it. Words can stay in a heart long after they're spoken, and I hate knowing mine may have left a mark on yours. You are the gentlest soul I know, and you deserve gentleness from me in return.

Please know this: none of it ever came from not caring. It came from me being imperfect — and that's no excuse, only the truth. You deserve better than my carelessness, and I want to be better for you.

My promise to you: I will listen more carefully, remember more faithfully, and choose my words more kindly. I will love you the way you deserve to be loved — with attention, with patience, and with my whole heart.

Thank you for still loving me through my flaws. I don't take you for granted — not for a second. I love you, and I'm going to keep proving it, every single day.

Rizky — sorry, and loving you always —
